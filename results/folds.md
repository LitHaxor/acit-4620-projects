## Key Diagnostic Findings

1. **Fold 3 is an extreme tail-variance outlier for ALL models (including non-visual baselines):** Even the constant Median Predictor jumps from `62.31–67.18` MAE on Folds 0/1/2/4 to **`74.33`** on Fold 3 (validation AQI standard deviation `108.20` vs `79.34–88.38` in other folds), and the GPS+month `E1_metadata_mlp` jumps from `38.11–42.98` to **`56.09`**.
2. **Station-grouped concentration of severe/hazardous episodes in Fold 3:** Although `StratifiedGroupKFold` balances total images (`1,624–1,654`), individual multi-image stations with extreme pollution episodes must stay intact in a single fold. In Fold 3, just four $1^\circ \times 1^\circ$ cells (`7` stations total) account for **`76.86%` (`176 / 229`)** of Fold 3's Bins 5–6 images: `(28, 77)` (Delhi/NCR, `85` images, mean AQI `288.84`, `79` tail = `34.50%`), `(25, -101)` (Saltillo/Monterrey, `51` images, mean AQI `474.00`, `47` Bin-6 Hazardous = `20.52%`), `(19, 99)` (Chiang Rai, `32` images, mean AQI `225.72`, `31` tail = `13.54%`), and `(18, 73)` (Pune/Maharashtra, `39` images, mean AQI `190.18`, `19` tail = `8.30%`).
